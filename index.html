Auditors don't have to check the queue; the queue notifies them
The moment a ticket is flagged, a message goes straight to the audit team's Slack/Teams channel, for example: 🔴 CR-0345 flagged: Rollback Plan missing (critical). @ahmad.rahman please review before end of day. Critical failures @-mention someone immediately; borderline scores are batched into a daily digest so the channel isn't spammed.
If unacknowledged after 4 hours, the alert automatically re-routes to whoever currently has the fewest open flags, so a ticket never sits waiting just because one auditor happens to be away or overloaded.
